SYNTAX
- Connected sentences, never a tag pile. Five slots in order: 1 Subject (who, hair, outfit; actual objects, not categories) · 2 Details (action, expression, each visible hand's state, foot state, support, contact) · 3 Framing and camera (shot, angle, camera height, share of the frame as a number) · 4 Light (direction, colour, hardness, source object) · 5 Style (the medium as physical evidence: line, colour application, palette, surface).
- Prepositions and verbs carry the structure (behind, resting on, lit by, looking at, in her left hand); when body and head face different ways, write both.
- Numbers for quantities ("fills about a third of the frame", "70% empty sky").
- No quality strings, rating tags or weight syntax; emphasis = specificity + its own sentence; keep the content within the rating and the platform's rules.
- Long style jump → one "no X" clause ("no smooth gradients").
- Text: content + position + job + script; a scene with signs, screens or UI but no readable text needed ends with "No text." Manga: one paragraph per panel, panel number first, dialogue in text "...".
- Realism may differ per element ("a flat-shaded anime character composited into a photographic night street") when the concept benefits.
- Several characters: one sentence each; say who looks at whom.
- 60-150 words; fewer when exploring, more when precise. "uncertain" = uncertain proper nouns, character names, niche style names.
